About Us



An honor to say "We, RNK Construction, built that with fidelity, purpose and integrity"

You Dream It, We Build It!

With over 10 years in land development, real estate, and medium to large-scale construction projects. RascelNathKyr Company is located in Tagaytay City, Mendez Cavite and Lemery Batangas with active office and site personnel.

Starting from a freelancing background, RascelNathKyr Construction kicked off in 2017. The name might sound a bit puzzling, but it’s a blend of the owner’s name, Rascel, and her two kids, Nathan and Kyrie.

We RNK Construction Company can turn your dream property into reality. Utilizing creative minds and exerting utmost effort toward quality work.

Vision & Mission

RascelNathKyr Construction Company is committed to providing reliable, quality construction services to clients. We strive to build and design structures that are not only aesthetically pleasing but also functional and durable.

Our Founders

RascelNathKyr Construction Company was built on vision, heart, and hands-on experience. Leading the company are its CEO and COO, seasoned professionals with over 10 years of expertise in land development, real estate, and medium to large-scale construction projects.

Ms. Florentina Vergara also known as Rascel is the sole owner of the company. She is a former General Manager on a construction company before she started her own. Through study and experience on engineering, she was able vision her dream.
